    Details and dialogs

    Details and summary

    What does plan do?

    It compares your joomiz.yaml with what exists and lists the changes. It changes nothing.

    What does apply do?

    It runs the plan that you reviewed. Each provisioner creates or changes its resources.

    What is drift?

    Drift is a difference between what the file says and what really exists.

    Dialog and popover

    Apply this plan?

    Two resources will be created in prod. You cannot undo this from the CLI.

    Tip

    A popover closes when you press Escape or click outside it.
