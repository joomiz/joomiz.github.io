Brand colours

Five colours come from the Joomiz brand guide. Each one is a fixed step in a ramp below.

Colour ramps

Each ramp has eleven steps, from 50 (lightest) to 950 (darkest). The blue and neutral ramps pass through the brand colours. The green, amber and red ramps are for status only. They are not brand colours.

Theme roles

A role says what a colour is for. Components use roles and never use a ramp step directly. Switch the theme and watch these values change.

Contrast

These ratios are measured live, for the theme that you see now. Text needs 4.5 : 1 or more. The edge of a control and the focus ring need 3 : 1 or more (WCAG 2.2 AA).
