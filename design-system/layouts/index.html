Layouts

A layout is the shell of a page: where the header, the menu and the content go. A page picks one and fills its slots. Preview shows a layout with sample content, on a desktop or on a screen the size of a phone.

Header and body

A list or a form with one column of content.

Slots: header_end, content, after. The file is src/layouts/header-body.html.

Open on its own page

Header, menu and body

A page that belongs to one thing and has sections to move between.

Slots: header_end, menu, content. The file is src/layouts/header-sidebar.html.

Open on its own page

Menu and body

A page of sections to move between, with no header. Use it for a page that sits under the header of another page.

Slots: menu, content. The file is src/layouts/sidebar-body.html.
